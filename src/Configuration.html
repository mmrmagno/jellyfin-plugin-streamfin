StreamFin inside your libraries

Puts a StreamFin entry at the top of a library, leading to online titles of the chosen kind. The library's own files and scans are not changed.

Content filters

Anime only, based on AniList tags, not an age rating. Movies & TV never shows titles TMDB marks as adult.

Results from StreamFin are tagged Streamable. Turning this off only hides them from search; the channels still work.

Anime sources

Open a title, then pick a source. Test looks up a well-known show on the site, then its episode list and the first episode's video, so a pass means playing works. It can take up to a minute.

Playback

Only a site that lists exactly this title, with exactly one episode of the same number. Watched state stays on the episode you picked.
Anime and TV shows: while an episode plays, the next one (on the same site) is looked up in the background, so it starts at once. Some sites take half a minute to start an episode otherwise.

Movies & TV

Make a free account on themoviedb.org, then go to Settings > API. The short API key and the long read access token both work. The channel stays hidden until a key is set.

Video sources

FlixHQ: the videos

Test the key after saving it. Each video test finds a well-known film and its video. When you press play, every site that is switched on is asked at once. The best answer plays, and the others are listed as versions in the player, named by site, quality and codec. A site that is down is skipped.

Quality

The tallest picture within this limit plays first. Taller versions stay in the version list.
H.264 plays on almost every device without the server converting it. HEVC versions are used when nothing else matches.

Play songs from

The first source that has the song plays it; the arrows set the order. Songs played before always come from this server's disk first. The state of each source updates every 10 seconds; switches and order take effect when you press Save.
  1. From the catalog address above. When it is down or slow, the next source plays the song at once.

  2. YouTube's official audio, fetched whole into the song cache (a new song takes 15 to 20 seconds). Ranked first, Monochrome is only asked when YouTube does not have the song. This needs yt-dlp and deno on the server: StreamFin downloads both from their official GitHub releases into its data folder, checks each against its published SHA-256, and updates them daily (Scheduled Tasks, "Update YouTube tools").

Find music with

Searches go to the first source. The next one answers when the first is down or takes longer than 6 seconds. Albums and artists always come from the source they were found in.
  1. Monochrome catalog: always on

  2. Its songs play from Monochrome's copy of the same recording, or from YouTube.

Song cache

Songs you play are kept on this server, so skipping within a song and playing it again are instant. The least recently played make room when it is full. Starred songs, songs in playlists and songs played more than once are kept ready as FLAC whenever Monochrome answers (up to half the cache), so they play even while it is down. 0 turns it off.
While a song plays, the next one in the app's play queue (or on the album) is fetched into the song cache, so it starts at once. When songs come from YouTube (Monochrome is down, or YouTube is first), opening an album also fetches its first song. Needs the song cache.

YouTube tools

YouTube refuses many data centre addresses ("Sign in to confirm you're not a bot"). Every YouTube search and download then goes through this proxy instead, for example a VPN container next to Jellyfin: http or socks5, with a host and port. Nothing else StreamFin fetches uses it. Needs the song cache.

Radio

Radio mixes an artist with similar ones, as ListenBrainz knows them. For artists ListenBrainz has no data on, a free key from last.fm/api is used as well.

Save starred albums as files

Your music libraries' folders. StreamFin saves into a StreamFin folder inside the one you pick. Choose "Another folder" to type a path.
Albums you mark as a favourite (the heart on an album in the Music channel, or a star in a Subsonic app) are saved here as tagged FLAC files, one folder per artist and album, so they play offline and in any music app. Use a folder that belongs to a Jellyfin music library and that Jellyfin can write to. Existing files are never replaced. Albums are saved slowly, one song every 20 seconds, to stay within the catalog's limits. Leave empty to save nothing.

    Subsonic apps

    For Symfonium, Feishin, Tempo, play:Sub and other Subsonic apps. In the app, use the server address , the Jellyfin username, and the app password below. The Jellyfin password does not work there. A new password replaces the old one.
    Some apps, Amperfy among them, ask for MP3 by default. With this on they get the original FLAC instead, which sounds better and seeks faster. A data saving limit in the app, such as 128 or 320 kbps, still gets a smaller converted file.

    Playlist import

    Every allowed user can bring playlists in from Spotify, YouTube Music, Deezer, a song list or a CSV export, on their own page: . Pasting a playlist link into Jellyfin's search imports it too. Imported playlists appear under Music > Playlists and in Subsonic apps.
    Spotify links are read from Spotify's public embed player, not an official API, up to 100 songs per playlist. With this off, users can still paste songs copied from the Spotify app.

    Cache and catalogs

    Clearing empties StreamFin's catalog and source caches and makes Jellyfin rebuild its saved listings. Warming loads the first pages of Anime and Movies & TV now; this also runs at startup and every six hours.

    Clearing removes found titles from every channel's Search results, for every user. Watched status and resume positions are kept.
