StreamFin

Browse anime in the Anime channel and movies and shows in Movies & TV, then play them with Jellyfin's own player.

Anime

Open a title and choose Episodes · HiAnime, AnimeGG or AnimeHeaven. If one source fails, return to the title and choose another.

Movies & TV

Free from themoviedb.org → Settings → API. Either the API key or the read access token works. The channel stays hidden until a key is set.

Content filters

These filters use AniList classifications and apply to anime browsing, search, and playback. They do not classify every scene and are not an age rating. Movies & TV always leaves out titles TMDB marks as adult.

Who can watch

Only selected users can browse or play. With no users selected, access is disabled.

AniList and TMDB provide catalog metadata. Availability depends on the enabled providers. Source changes can temporarily interrupt playback.
