Play songs from

The first source that has the song plays it; the arrows set the order. Songs played before always come from this server's disk first. The state of each source updates every 10 seconds; switches and order take effect when you press Save.
  1. From the catalog address above. When it is down or slow, the next source plays the song at once.

  2. YouTube's official audio, fetched whole into the song cache (a new song takes 15 to 20 seconds). Ranked first, Monochrome is only asked when YouTube does not have the song. This needs yt-dlp and deno on the server: StreamFin downloads both from their official GitHub releases into its data folder, checks each against its published SHA-256, and updates them daily (Scheduled Tasks, "Update YouTube tools").

Albums you mark as a favourite (the heart on an album in the Music channel, or a star in a Subsonic app) are saved here as tagged FLAC files, one folder per artist and album, so they play offline and in any music app. Use a folder that belongs to a Jellyfin music library and that Jellyfin can write to. Existing files are never replaced. Albums are saved slowly, one song every 20 seconds, to stay within the catalog's limits. Leave empty to save nothing.

    Subsonic apps

    For Symfonium, Feishin, Tempo, play:Sub and other Subsonic apps. In the app, use the server address , the Jellyfin username, and the app password below. The Jellyfin password does not work there. A new password replaces the old one.
    Some apps, Amperfy among them, ask for MP3 by default. With this on they get the original FLAC instead, which sounds better and seeks faster. A data saving limit in the app, such as 128 or 320 kbps, still gets a smaller converted file.
